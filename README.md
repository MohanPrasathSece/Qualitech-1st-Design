# Qualitech Landing Page

Create ONLY the HOME PAGE for QUALITECH CONNECTRONICS PRIVATE LIMITED using the uploaded Qualitech logo.

Design it as a premium industrial/electronics engineering website — modern, minimal, sophisticated and highly professional.

THEME:
- Metallic grey + white as the dominant colors
- Graphite/dark charcoal for contrast
- Use the logo's blue and orange only as subtle accents
- Premium metallic/brushed-steel feel
- Lots of clean white space
- Typography: Manrope for headings/navigation, Inter for body text
- Strong, elegant typography and precise spacing

STYLE:
Think premium precision engineering, electronics manufacturing and advanced industrial technology. Avoid generic corporate/WordPress styling, excessive gradients, excessive dark sections or flashy effects.

HERO:
Create a powerful split-layout hero with a high-quality cable assembly/connector/manufacturing image.

Small label:
"ESTABLISHED 1995"

Headline:
"Precision Connections.
Engineered for Performance."

Subheading:
"Custom Wire & Cable Harness Solutions for Demanding Industries"

Description:
"Qualitech Connectronics delivers high-quality custom-built wire and cable harnesses, cable assemblies and connector solutions for OEM applications."

Buttons:
"EXPLORE OUR SOLUTIONS"
"REQUEST A QUOTE"

Add subtle animated technical/circuit lines around the product image.

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS:

1. EXPERIENCE / STATS
- 1995 — Established
- 30+ — Years of Experience
- OEM — Focused Solutions
- 4 — Key Industries

Use elegant animated counters.

2. ABOUT QUALITECH
Heading:
"Built on Experience. Driven by Precision."

Briefly introduce the company, established in 1995, its experienced manpower, technology, custom cable harnesses and OEM focus.

Use a large real factory/cable image with smooth scroll reveal.

3. WHAT WE DO
Create 3 premium interactive cards:
- Cable Assemblies
- Connectors
- Facilities

Cards should have real imagery, subtle metallic borders and hover effects:
image zoom → card lift → blue accent line → arrow movement.

4. INDUSTRIES
Heading:
"Solutions for Critical Industries"

Show four premium image cards:
Telecommunications
Power
Defense
Railways

5. WHY QUALITECH
Show 5 clean features:
Application-Specific
Quality Focused
Experienced Team
Competitive Solutions
Timely Supply

6. FACILITIES PREVIEW
Large factory/manufacturing image with:
"Technology Meets Expertise"
and button:
"EXPLORE OUR FACILITIES →"

7. AUTHORIZED & SUPPORTED PRODUCTS
Create a smooth horizontal scrolling showcase using the supplied product/partner images. Pause scrolling on hover.

8. FINAL CTA
Dark graphite/metallic section:

"Looking for a Custom Cable Solution?"

"Tell us about your application and requirements."

Buttons:
"REQUEST A QUOTE"
"CONTACT US"

Show:
+91-40-27140004
info@qualitechindia.in

9. FOOTER
Minimal premium dark graphite footer with logo, navigation, product categories and contact details.

ANIMATIONS:
Use subtle premium animations only:
- hero text reveal
- image reveal
- scroll fade/slide
- animated counters
- image zoom on hover
- card lift
- technical connection-line animations
- smooth button/arrow transitions
- subtle parallax

Animations must feel precise and expensive, not flashy.

Make the homepage fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.

Use real Qualitech images wherever available. Do not invent certifications, clients, awards or technical specifications.

The final result should feel like a premium international industrial engineering company website: WHITE + METALLIC GREY + GRAPHITE + subtle QUALITECH BLUE/ORANGE accents, with Manrope typography and sophisticated engineering animations.
